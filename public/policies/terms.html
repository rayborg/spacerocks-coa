1. Service

The service submits the exact 32-byte manifest SHA-256 digest to independent public OpenTimestamps calendars, monitors proof upgrades, verifies a resulting Bitcoin attestation, retains the proof package, and provides private status and proof download. Automated email notices are not currently enabled. Checkout displays the current managed service price before payment. Stripe controls payment collection; the server controls the Price and amount.

2. What the proof means

A valid proof establishes that the submitted digest existed no later than the attested Bitcoin block. It does not prove authenticity, ownership, authorship, physical custody, exact creation time, or the truth of certificate content.

An initial verified status may become available after at least one canonical Bitcoin confirmation. Monitoring continues through at least six confirmations, and the retained proof may be upgraded as confirmation evidence matures. Recent Bitcoin blocks can reorganize; conflicting or reduced evidence is moved to manual review rather than represented as final.

3. Timing and availability
