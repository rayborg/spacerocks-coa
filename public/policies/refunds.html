Managed Timestamp Refund Policy

This policy applies to the optional Spacerocks managed timestamp service.

Eligible refunds

Contact support within 14 days of payment for review if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same intended order;
  • payment succeeded but the service did not submit the digest to any configured calendar within 72 hours;
  • the submitted digest or Price differs from the server-bound order because of a verified service error; or
  • a refund is required by applicable law.

After calendar acceptance

A calendar or Bitcoin commitment is irreversible. A refund cannot delete it or guarantee that independent systems stop retaining it.

After valid calendar submission, the managed processing fee is generally non-refundable except for duplicate charges, a verified service error, or rights provided by law. Calendar delay, Bitcoin confirmation time, an email provider delay, or loss of a customer-held status token does not by itself establish refund eligibility.

How to request a refund

Email borgesraymond@gmail.com with the opaque order reference, payment date, and reason. Do not send card details, private keys, passphrases, full certificate packages, or status tokens. Approved refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method; bank processing time varies.

Disputes

Please contact support before opening a payment dispute so service evidence can be reviewed. This request does not limit any legal or cardholder rights.
